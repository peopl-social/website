---
title: About peopl.
description: Why we're building a social app for a handful of people instead of an audience.
---

Most social apps are built around an audience. You post, strangers react, and a feed decides what your friends see. The people you'd actually call at 2am end up scattered across group chats and stories.

peopl. is for them. Each person keeps one board. You choose who gets in. Plans get a place of their own, and messages are end-to-end encrypted.

## What we care about

**Small on purpose.** peopl. is built for close friends, not followers. There are no public like counts and no feed to scroll.

**Real life first.** The app should help you see people, then get out of the way.

**Honest about where we are.** peopl. is in early access. Today the waitlist is the only part that exists. The rest is being built.
