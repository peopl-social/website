**Last updated: September 2026 — early access**

peopl. is in early access, so this page is intentionally short. Here is what happens today, plainly.

## The waitlist

When you join the early list, we store your email address and the date you joined. We use it to send peopl. updates and early-access invites. That is the whole list — no analytics profiles, no data bazaar, no third parties.

You can leave at any time by emailing [support@peopl.social](mailto:support@peopl.social) and we will delete your address.

## The app

peopl. is not publicly available yet, so there are no user accounts or user content to speak of. When it launches, this page will describe exactly how boards, gatherings, and messages are handled — before the app goes live, not after.

The short version of the design goal: your conversations are end-to-end encrypted and travel through a plaintext-blind relay, meaning the server carries them without being able to read them.

## Questions

Email [support@peopl.social](mailto:support@peopl.social) and a person will answer.
